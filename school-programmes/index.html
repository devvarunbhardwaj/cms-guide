01

How it works

School = what the website lists

School pages, the schools on /programs and /academics, the school filter, breadcrumbs and "related programmes" all follow the school that picked the programme.

Discipline = marketing, unchanged

The programme's web address /programs/<discipline>/<programme> and the LeadSquared form still use Discipline. Moving a programme to another school never changes its address.

Not picked yet?

A programme no school has picked stays with the school of its discipline, as it is today. So nothing disappears while you work through the schools.

Example

  • B.Tech Biotechnology has Discipline Biotechnology.
  • Add it to School of Engineering → Programs.
  • It now shows on the School of Engineering page, with "School of Engineering" in its breadcrumb.
  • Its address stays /programs/biotechnology/btech-biotechnology-engineering, and the form still pre-fills Biotechnology.
02

Add programmes to a school

Open Data · School ↗ and click the school.

Fields

on Data · School
FieldWhat to put

In Programs, type a programme name and pick it. Add all of the school's programmes. Drag them to set the order: the school page lists them in this order.

Click Save, then Publish (top right).

Add every programme the school should show, not just the ones moving in. Programmes you have not picked yet still show (from their discipline) after the ones you picked.

A programme with Show on website off is skipped, even if a school picked it.

03 · only when needed

Slug: the school page's address

Leave it empty and the school keeps its address today, /programs/<discipline>, e.g. /programs/engineering.

Set it only when a school needs its own address: a new school, or two schools on the same discipline.

Example: School of Travel & Tourism

needs a Slug
FieldValue

It shares Hotel Management and Tourism with School of Hospitality, so today it has no page of its own. With this Slug it gets /programs/school-of-travel-and-tourism.

Lower-case words joined by hyphens. Changing a Slug later moves the page and breaks old links, so set it once.

04

Rules checked on Publish to website

SituationResultFix

"Stops" means nothing goes to the website and the message names the schools. The live site stays as it was.

05

Publish to website

Left menu → Website → Publish to website. Changes are live in a few minutes.

You can do the schools one at a time. Each publish shows how many programmes are still in no school.
