Filling the Our Identity page

Sections 01 Our Seal and 02 How to use the Seal? on /about/our-identity come from the our_identity_blocks list in About page. Each section is one entry in that list. The logo on the left is a separate field (identity_logo) and is already filled.

How

Steps

01

Content Manager › Single types › About page.

Find our_identity_blocks.

Good to know

What each field means

anchor_id

Internal ID, not shown. Lowercase letters and dashes only. Don't change it later.

label

A short name for the block. Required, max 40 characters.

title

The big heading of the section.

index

The small number above the heading: 01, 02.

cards

Boxes with a title and a paragraph. cols sets how many per row.

list

Bullet points. One entry = one bullet.

Leave the other fields empty: intro, tabs, numbered, list_icons, inline_icons, image, image_wide. The order of the two entries is the order on the page, so Block 1 goes first (drag the handle to reorder).
