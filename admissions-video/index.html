The Overview video on admission pages

The video is shown only after it has a cover photo.

01

Show the video

Open Page · Admissions ↗

Go to the plates list, then + Add an entry:

FieldValue

Need the video on other admission pages too? Add one entry per page, using that page's key:

Pagekey
02 · optional

Change the video

Open Page · Infrastructure ↗

Field campus_film_id: paste only the YouTube ID, which is the part after v=.

Example: youtube.com/watch?v=oXyLEEPTPdk → oXyLEEPTPdk

All admission pages play this one video.
